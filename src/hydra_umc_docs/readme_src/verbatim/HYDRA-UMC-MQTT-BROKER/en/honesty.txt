**Honesty check - what actually runs today:** the broker transport (`src/server.ts`), the topic ACL (`src/acl.ts`), and the CONNECT authentication (`src/auth.ts`) are real and tested ({tests} tests passing across 12 files), and `tsc --noEmit` is clean. These aren't isolated unit tests against mocked internals - `tests/server.test.ts` connects a real `mqtt` client library over a real TCP socket to a real running Aedes broker, and that's how a real bug (Aedes 1.x's `broker.listen()` step being skipped, silently hanging every CONNECT) was actually found. The MQTT-over-WebSocket listener and the payload-size limit are the same way: real, opt-in, and broker-tested. The `hydra/swarm/...` topics are published by the MQTT bridge of HYDRA-UMC-SERVER (off by default; its `docs/MQTT_BRIDGE.md`) and the `hydra/bridges/<name>/...` topics by the 5 external machine bridges; `tests/discovery-broker.test.ts` runs the swarm topics under the proposed ACL against a real broker. The mDNS announcement and the Home Assistant configs of the bridges are real but opt-in and off by default (`MQTT_MDNS=1`, `MQTT_HOME_ASSISTANT=1`); they are checked with a real broker and a simulated network, not on a real network with multicast or a real Home Assistant. See `CHANGELOG.md` for exactly what has shipped so far.

---

> **Safety and security status:** this ecosystem is not a certified safety system. There is no hardware E-STOP path, no firmware-enforced limits or interlocks and no verified motion command yet; firmware images are checked with CRC-32 and an Ed25519 signature, and a release build refuses the published development keys (the tool heads and expansion boards behind the relay still run URTC bootloaders, which take a shared-key HMAC-SHA256 instead of a signature); TLS and mutual TLS are optional and off by default; no custom board has been populated or tested yet. Read the [security baseline](https://github.com/JuanenRac/HYDRA-UMC-DOCS/blob/main/docs/SECURITY_BASELINE.md) and the [threat model](https://github.com/JuanenRac/HYDRA-UMC-DOCS/blob/main/docs/THREAT_MODEL.md) before relying on any statement here for the safety of a person.
